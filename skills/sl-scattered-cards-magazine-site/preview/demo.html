About the journal

What does The Side Margin cover?

We publish reported stories about creative spaces, working methods, and the people behind useful, lasting work.

How often are new stories published?

A new feature appears every two weeks. Shorter notes are added to the archive between features.

Can I suggest a story?

Yes. Send a brief note about the person, place, or practice you think we should cover.
