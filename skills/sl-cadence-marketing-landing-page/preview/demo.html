Before you begin

Do I need to connect my publishing tools?

No. You can plan and review a launch in Pacefield without integrations. Publishing still happens in your existing channel tools.

Can I invite a reviewer who is not on my team?

Yes. Invite them as a collaborator and assign them the assets they need to review. Collaborators count toward your plan limit.

What happens if I leave Studio?

Your campaigns remain available. You can keep one campaign active on Free and archive the others until you need them.
