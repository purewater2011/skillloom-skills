04Learning Loop Deck

Each cycle turns a real decision into a chance to improve.

Notice

Spot the decision point in a short service scenario.

Try

Choose and explain a response before seeing the model.

Feedback

Compare the response with two observable criteria.

Revise

Retry a similar case with one deliberate change.

05Learning Loop Deck

Four weeks create repeated chances to apply feedback.

Week 1

Baseline scenario and first coached attempt.

Week 2

Two short cases with immediate criterion-based feedback.

Week 3

Paired practice on less predictable cases.

Week 4

Independent case and review of remaining errors.

06Learning Loop Deck

The pilot changes what learners do between explanations.

Current sequence

  • Read the procedure once
  • Discuss an example as a group
  • Complete one final check

Proposed loop

  • Attempt a decision before the explanation
  • Receive feedback on visible criteria
  • Retry with a deliberate adjustment
